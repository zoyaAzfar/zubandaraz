Play the Game

Play 'The Master's Tools' here.

The rules of the game are simple! Live through a single day in the life of the wordless main character. Can you complete the tasks on your to-do lists in time while keeping everyone happy? Or will you succumb to the pressure?

This game was designed to communicate everyday life and its associated challenges for typical working women in Pakistan. Briefly, this game highlights how a typical professional woman faces multiple personal and professional challenges throughout her typical day. The game design was informed by relevant academic scholarship and use examples taken or inspired by the interviews we conducted for our proverb and word collection to allow users to experience what it may feel like to hear these words in a day.

This game was adopted into the Master's course MBA6060: Managing Workplace Diversity at the Lahore University of Management Sciences.
