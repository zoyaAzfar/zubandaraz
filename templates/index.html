WHO WE ARE

Zuban Daraz [adj.]: big-mouthed, arrogant, disrespectful in speech.

Or, more literally: a drawer (daraz) of language (zuban).

Zuban Daraz is a sociolinguistics project dedicated to exploring the relationship of South Asian women with language. Through anonymous submissions, we document examples of words which play a key role in how women are referred to - and percieved - by those around them, across cultures and language. These words do not necessarily need to be insults or taboo words; instead, we are looking for words that hold special significance for you. By exploring how can hold different meanings for different people, we hope to get a glimpse into the unique stories of women in South Asia.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Pick a word

Choose a word of your choice which you feel is derogatory or with which you have a complex relationship. This doesn't have to be a profanity, it could be an ordinary word in any language of your choice.

02

Tell us about it

Write about what the word means literally and what connotations it holds. And, if you're comfortable, if there was a specific moment in your life that motivated you to pick it.

03

Submit!

Publish your (totally anonymous) submission to our dictionary. Once it gets approved by our moderators, it'll get listed here for people to see.
